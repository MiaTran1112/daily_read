### Stats review

- p-value:
    - p-value is the probability of observing the value of the calculated test statistic under the null hypothesis assumptions

- type I and II errors:
    - type I error, which is also known as a “false positive”
    - type II error, which is also known as a “false negative.”

- bernoulli distribution:
    -the discrete probability distribution of a random variable which takes the value 1 with probability p and the value 0 with probability q=1−p
