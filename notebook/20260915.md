1. More statisitcal concepts to review

- Standard deviation vs variance
    - Both — how spread out the values are around the mean.
    - SD: negative or positive
    - Variance: the scale/significance/how far the values are around the mean

- Covariance: measures how two variables move together (positive = move in same direction; negative = move in opposite directions).

- LLN, CLT
    - Law of Large Numbers (LLN): As the sample size n grows, the sample mean gets closer to the true population mean.
    - Central Limit Theorem (CLT): No matter the shape of the population distribution, the sampling distribution of the sample mean becomes approximately normal when n is large
