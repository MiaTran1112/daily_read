3. Stats concepts:
- Correlation vs causation
- Confounding factor
- Selection bias vs Sampling bias:
    - Selection bias: broader category/umbrella term that includes sampling bias. "occurs when individuals or groups in a study differ systematically from the population of interest leading to a systematic error in an association or outcome."
    - Sampling bias: a type of selection bias. when the sample isn't representative of the target population, leading to skewed results
- Regression
- Over/underfitting
- Crossvalidation
- Statitiscal significance:
    - how surprising the observed result would be under the null hypothesis, given the model and assumptions. It doesn't tell us that the effect is large or economically important
- t-test/z-score
- Types of error: 1/2
