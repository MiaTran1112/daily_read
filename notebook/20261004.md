**Google: How would you derive a confidence interval for the probability of flipping heads from a series of coin tosses?**
- Use formula:
    p_hat +- (z_alpha/2)*sqrt[(p_hat)*(1-P_hat)/n)]
where p_hat is the proportion of heads tossed in series, and n is the series size. The Cl is centered at the series proportion, and plus or minus a margin of error.

**Two Sigma: What is the expected number of coin flips needed to get two consecutive heads?**
Way 1: Let x be the expected number of coin flips needed. We can break down the possible scenarios:
1. If a tail appears on the first flip:
Probability: 1/2
Additional flips needed: x+1
2. If a head appears on the first flip and a tail appears on the second flip:
Probability: 1/4
Additional flips needed: x+2
3. If we get two consecutive heads:
Probability: 1/4
Total flips needed: 2
-> E[X] = 1/2(x+1) + 1/4(x+2) + 1/4*2
-> E[X] = 6
